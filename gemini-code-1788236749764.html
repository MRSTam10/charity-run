Event Introduction / 賽事簡介

【Run to Mei Shan cum little Tam Tam Charity Challenge】

The "Run to Mei Shan cum little Tam Tam Charity Challenge" is a meaningful charity running event driven by love, nature, and perseverance. The primary objective of this event is to bring together community support to secure long-term protection and daily living assistance for little Tam Tam.

This event goes far beyond the confines of a traditional running track. We invite runners to step into nature, traverse majestic mountains and rivers, and push beyond their personal boundaries. Inspired by the vision of "Stepping into Beautiful Mountains ('Mei Shan') to Overcome Boundaries", participants will experience both the beauty of nature and the joy of self-transcendence.

「走進美山暨小謙謙慈善挑戰賽」是一項結合自然越野與大愛的慈善跑步活動。本次賽事的成立宗旨，是希望集合社會各界的溫暖與力量，為「小謙謙」的日常生活與未來成長提供一份堅實的保障與支持。

這項比賽不單限於一般的運動場跑道，我們將帶領每一位參加者步出市區，走進美麗的山河與大自然之中。在山水包羅萬象的考驗下，讓跑手們踏出舒適圈、跨越自己的極限——這正是「走進美山、超越自我」的真正意義。
